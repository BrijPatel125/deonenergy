# Hosting — Deon Energy Website

Site is WordPress. One website, no e-commerce, no logins. Both options below run it comfortably.

**Important: buy the entry-level / cheapest plan that allows 1 website.** Our site needs under 1 GB of space. Higher plans exist for people hosting 10, 50, 100 sites — we don't need them.

---

## Option 1 — Hostinger

**Plan:** Web Hosting or WordPress Hosting — lowest tier that supports 1 website
**Price:** roughly ₹70–₹150/month, billed yearly (check live page, promos change weekly)

**Good**
- Cheapest of the two for the same job
- Indian servers (Mumbai) — faster for Indian visitors
- Free SSL, one-click WordPress, simple control panel (hPanel)
- Free automatic migration if you ever move hosts

**Watch out**
- Renewal price is higher than the first-term price. Buying 2–3 years upfront locks the low rate.
- Support is chat only, no phone.

**Link:** https://www.hostinger.in/web-hosting

---

## Option 2 — GoDaddy

**Plan:** Web Hosting Starter / Economy, or Managed WordPress lowest tier
**Price:** roughly ₹90–₹300/month, billed yearly (check live page)

**Good**
- Biggest brand, easiest for a non-technical team to deal with directly
- 24/7 phone support with an India number — call instead of typing
- If you already own the domain here, hosting + domain sit in one account

**Watch out**
- Costs more than Hostinger for the same specs
- Heavy upsells at checkout. Decline the SEO tool, extra security, website builder, extra email packs. Not needed.
- Renewal price also jumps after the first term.

**Link:** https://in.godaddy.com/hosting/web-hosting

## Which plan tier?

Whatever the plan page shows, the only things that matter for us:

| Need | Us |
|---|---|
| Websites | 1 |
| Storage | Under 1 GB used |
| SSL | Required — free on both |
| PHP + MySQL | Standard, on every plan |

Anything beyond that is upsell.

Nice-to-have on higher tiers, not required:
- **Staging site** — useful later for testing changes, not needed at launch
- **Free domain 1st year** — worth ~₹800–1,000. If the upgrade costs more than that, skip it.
